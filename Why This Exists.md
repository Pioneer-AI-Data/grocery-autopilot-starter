# Why This Exists

We were overspending on groceries and could feel it, but could not see it. Multiple store runs a week, a cart that filled itself, meals stuck in a rut, and a "what's for dinner" question that landed on the same person every night at 5 PM.

So we pointed AI at it. The receipts told the story in one afternoon: about a third of every trip was snacks, desserts, and drinks. Fruit and vegetables were worth protecting. The in-store average was $136 a trip; the first planned pickup order was $54.

The system that came out of it is this kit:

- The pantry is photographed, not memorized.
- The week is planned around the family calendar, so busy nights get 15-minute dinners instead of takeout.
- The list is priced before anyone shops, and the order rides to the store's pickup lane, where end-caps cannot reach you.
- Every receipt gets logged, so the budget is a trend you watch instead of a fight you lose.
- The picky kid always has a listed path, so dinner is never a standoff.

None of it requires new apps for the family. The shared Reminders list they already use is the front door; the AI does the bookkeeping.
