# How the Grocery List Works (the family guide)

> Written for the household, not the computer. Keep it true: when a rule changes, this changes.

## The one list

- The list is **[list name]** in the Reminders app.
- Add anything any time: "Hey Siri, add ketchup to my [list name] list," or type it.
- Plain names ("Ketchup"), details in the item notes. Nothing added gets lost.

## What happens every [planning day]

- Someone snaps 30 seconds of fridge/freezer/pantry photos.
- The dinner week gets planned around everyone's evenings and lands where the family can see it.
- The list gets priced, approved once, and picked up. Receipt gets photographed.

## Changing things (yes, you can)

- Edit the list any time. Swap a dinner by saying so. "We skipped tacos" reshuffles the week, nothing wasted.
- Any preference you say out loud becomes a permanent rule the same day.
