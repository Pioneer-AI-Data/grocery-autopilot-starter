# The Weekly Loop

The whole system, one week at a time. Total human effort: about two minutes plus one yes.

1. **Plan-day morning**: the household gets the nudge (text, if you set up the notifier).
2. **Photos**: 30 seconds of fridge, freezer, pantry into Claude Code. Add anything the pantry cannot show: "we skipped Thursday", "the girls are sick of wraps."
3. **"Plan the week"**: the dinner week appears, built around the calendars, the weekend, the rules, and what you already own. Correct it in plain English; corrections become permanent.
4. **The list**: priced (live, with the API), family additions swept in, pushed to the shared Reminders list.
5. **One yes**: the resolved cart, item by item with prices, goes to your Kroger cart for pickup. Or shop the Reminders list however you like.
6. **Pickup**: whoever is driving picks the slot and pays in the store's app.
7. **Receipt photo**: spending logged, off-list creep visible, pantry restocked on paper.

Repeat. By week three the meal library knows your family, the staples table knows your prices, and the monthly rollup shows the trend. The trend is the whole point.
