# Start Here

Twenty minutes, four steps, and your groceries run themselves. Do these in order.

## 1. Make the rules yours (10 minutes)

Open `Household/Grocery System.md` and fill in every [bracketed] blank:

- Your monthly budget target (treat it as a direction to work toward, not a cap)
- Your stores and what each is for
- **Which calendars count.** List the exact names of the family calendars that hold real commitments. Leave work calendars and imported sports feeds out; only events someone typed in should shape dinner.
- Who is picky about what, allergies, dislikes, and anything you grow at home (never buy what the garden makes)
- Your planning day (pick the weekday you are reliably near a computer)

## 2. Point it at your list (2 minutes)

Type the exact name of your family's shared grocery list into `Grocery System.md` (Apple Reminders on a Mac gets automatic writes; on Windows see `Guides/Windows Setup.md`). If you do not have one: make a list in Reminders, share it with your family, done. Two rules that matter:

- Item names stay plain ("Ketchup") so the list auto-sorts by store section
- Details go in the item's notes, never in parentheses in the name

## 3. First inventory (5 minutes)

Open this folder in Claude Code. Photograph your fridge, freezer, and pantry (blurry is fine), drop the photos into the chat, and say **"update the pantry."** Then tell it what your family actually eats for dinner: ten meals is plenty to start.

## 4. First plan (3 minutes)

Say **"plan the week."** Review the week it proposes, correct anything ("no fish", "Tuesday we're out late"), and every correction becomes a permanent rule. Approve the list and shop it however you like.

## The power-up (optional, 15 minutes, worth it)

`Guides/Kroger API Setup.md` walks you through a free Kroger developer account. After it, every list prices itself at your store, sales get flagged, and approved orders load straight into your Kroger cart for pickup. This is where the money savings get real: pickup orders kill impulse buying.

## The rhythm

`Guides/The Weekly Loop.md` describes the whole cadence: one nudge, one batch of photos, one yes per week. Everything else is the system's job.
